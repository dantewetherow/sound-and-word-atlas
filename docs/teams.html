Steps · Letters · Letter teams

Letter teams

Two or three letters that work together. Each team is drawn in its colours, with the bond bar that joins its letters and its sound circle. Tap a circle for the key word: ow as in snow, ow as in cow.

The colours are the notation’s. Red letters are vowels, and a red bar joins a vowel team. Green is for sh, ch and th, and for tch, where the t stays black. Black bars join consonant teams. An r-controlled unit keeps its vowel red and its r black under one red bar. Qu has two sounds, one circle under each letter.

Some teams spell two sounds, and each sound gets its own tile. Ow is in snow and cow, oo in moon and book, ea in eat and head. Th is voiced in this (the buzzy th) and voiceless in thin. The step under a tile is where the path brings the team in.

To drill: choose the rows with the buttons at the top, then Drill. Each team comes up alone and large. In Say, then check, the first tap shows the key word and the second moves on. Fast moves on with each tap. A pace of 3, 2 or 1.2 seconds runs the drill on its own; tap to pause. The count and the time show at the end. Space or the right arrow moves on, the left arrow goes back, and Escape stops.
