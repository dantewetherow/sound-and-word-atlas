A family: note, notate, notation

Each word is built from the one before it. At each seam the e drops, and its job, making the o spell its name, passes to the next vowel.

What’s that ablaut?

write, wrote, written: the vowel changes to mark time, the silent e vaults, and a block box keeps the i of written short.

Squeezed into oblivionContractions: the vowel squeezed out, the apostrophe in its place, and the apostrophe catastrophes. write, wrote, writtenAblaut, the vault and the box in one family, with spoken and driven as the contrasts.
