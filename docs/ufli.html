The route and UFLI Foundations

Where each step of the route meets UFLI Foundations, by lesson number, so a school that runs UFLI can see where a step lands in its 128 lessons, and where the two orders part.

Made to line up with UFLI Foundations' scope and sequence, by lesson number. UFLI didn't make it and hasn't endorsed it. The descriptions are ours. UFLI asks that its materials be linked rather than copied (its copyright statement says so), so its lesson names stay on its own site: its scope and sequence is in the UFLI Foundations Toolbox.
