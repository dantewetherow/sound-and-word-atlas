Printing

Print in colour and double-sided, flipping on the long edge, so each coded word lands behind its plain one. Print one sheet first and hold it up to a window to check that the backs line up, then cut on the grey lines. Spaces left over on the last sheet are blank cards to write on.

The coding

The coded sides show the markings as they stand in the manual now. Some of these words still have marking calls open, and the cards will be rebuilt when those are ruled.

Where the words come from

The Early Literacy Profile (ELP) Assessment & Instructional Guide, Central Okanagan Public Schools (2025), pp. 16–17. The guide doesn't name a source for its lists; it says they were aligned with a phonics scope and sequence, keeping one word for each pattern. These cards aren't an SD23 publication. The district's own ELP page is the place for the assessment itself.
