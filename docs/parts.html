Parts

A dictionary files words by their first letter, which scatters a part across the alphabet. Here each part gathers its words, wherever it sits in them, so the meaning they share shows.

239 words are taken apart so far, 74 of them as a word sum only, with no marks yet. A word that’s marked opens in Words.

What happens to the spelling where two parts meet.

A consonant doubles16 words

The base ends in one vowel letter and one consonant, and the ending starts with a vowel. The consonant doubles so the box can keep the vowel short. Step 14 teaches it, with Carney’s pages.

  • bed + d + ing → bedding
  • cut + t + ing → cutting
  • fun + n + y → funny
