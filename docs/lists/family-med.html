Legomorphic spelling · One root, many words

The *med- family: mode, model, modern, module

*med- means to measure, to take fitting steps. Its spellings are mod (a measure, a manner: mode, accommodate) and med (to treat: medical).

This list goes with The *med- family.

ac + com + mod + ate → accommodateThe cabin can accommodate six people.
ac + com + mod + at(e) + ion → accommodationWe found accommodation by the lake.

The contrast

Two c’s and two m’s: ac + com + mod. Each prefix brings its own.

For the teacher

Accommodate is one of the most misspelled words in English. Its sum fixes it: ac + com + mod + ate, so two c’s and two m’s.

The check ends with modular: the e of module drops before -ar, as it does before -ate. English’s own word from the root, must, has no parts to build.

Have the learner build each word from its sum, not copy it, and say each sentence before writing it. Six to eight words is the whole list: the convention is what’s being learned.
