Step 32: A Greek family: photo, phone, graph

Words from Greek spell /f/ with ph, and are built from Greek parts: photo (light), phone (sound), graph (writing).

This list goes with Step 32: A Greek family: photo, phone, graph.

Answer the phone.

Did it generalize?

This list has a check: 11 words to give before the lesson, after it, and a few weeks later. Most of them aren’t on the list, so it shows whether the convention was learned or only the words.

Open the check

For the teacher

Alphabet is named for the first two Greek letters, alpha and beta. Words from Greek keep ph for /f/ (Carney, p. 228).

The check ends with fish: an English word, so its /f/ is f.

Have the learner build each word from its sum, not copy it, and say each sentence before writing it. Six to eight words is the whole list: the convention is what’s being learned.
