Step 22: A team's second sound

A team can say a second sound: oo in book, not moon; ea in head, not eat.

Print this list: the teacher’s notes are left off.

The words

Look up.
That was good.
Read a book.
My head hurts.
Bread and butter.
Put it here.
Pull the rope.
She said yes.

The contrast

A team can say two sounds.

Build it

  1. l + oo + k →
  2. h + ea + d →
  3. br + ea + d →
  4. p + u + t →
  5. p + u + ll →
  6. g + oo + d →

Use it

  1. Wear a hat on your (head or heed) .
  2. Can you (pull or pool) the wagon?
  3. Please (put or putt) it away.
  4. What a good (book or boot) !

Check: not on the list

  1. f + oo + t →foot
  2. br + ea + th →breath
  3. p + u + sh →push
  4. f + oo + d →food

For the teacher

Said keeps the spelling of say and shifts its vowel. The list say, pay, lay explains it.

The check ends with food, where oo says its first sound.

Have the learner say each sentence before writing it. Six to eight words is the whole list: the convention is what’s being learned.
