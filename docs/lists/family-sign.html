Legomorphic spelling · One root, many words

The sign family: sign, signal, signature, design

sign means a mark. Its g is quiet in sign, design and assign, and said in signal, signature and signify. The spelling keeps it everywhere, so the family stays in view.

This list goes with The sign family.

Word study: The g in sign, a presentation for learners.

Print this list: the teacher’s notes are left off.

The words

The sign says stop.
sign + al → signalWait for the signal.
sign + at(e) + ure → signatureWrite your signature here.
de + sign → designDesign a flag.
de + sign + er → designerShe is a designer.
as + sign → assignI will assign the jobs.
as + sign + ment → assignmentHand in your assignment.
re + sign → resignHe will resign from the team.
sign + i + fy → signifyWhat does a red light signify?
sign + i + fic + ant → significantIt was a significant day.

The contrast

The g is quiet in sign and said in signal: the i is long in one and short in the other.

For the teacher

Assign has two s’s because the prefix ad- became as- before the s of sign. Each part brings its own.

Seal is a cousin that lost its g: Latin sigillum, a little sign, through French seel.

Have the learner build each word from its sum, not copy it, and say each sentence before writing it. Six to eight words is the whole list: the convention is what’s being learned.
