Step 27: Two words join

Two words can join into one, with an apostrophe where letters are left out: I + am is I’m, do + not is don’t.

This list goes with Step 27: Two words join.

Print this list: the teacher’s notes are left off.

The words

I’mI + am → I’mI’m here.
it’sit + is → it’sIt’s late.
isn’tis + not → isn’tIt isn’t fair.
don’tdo + not → don’tDon’t run.
can’tcan + not → can’tI can’t see.
we’rewe + are → we’reWe’re ready.

The contrast

It’s is it is; its means belonging to it.

Join them

  1. I + am →
  2. it + is →
  3. do + not →
  4. is + not →
  5. we + are →
  6. can + not →

Use it

  1. (it is) raining.
  2. I (do not) know.
  3. She (is not) home.
  4. (we are) late!

Check: not on the list

  1. you + are →you’re
  2. did + not →didn’t
  3. he + is →he’s
  4. will + not →won’t

For the teacher

The apostrophe stands where letters were left out: one mark, wherever the gap is (Carney, p. 50).

The check ends with won’t, which changes its first word: will becomes wo. Learn it as a word.

Have the learner build each word from its sum, not copy it, and say each sentence before writing it. Six to eight words is the whole list: the convention is what’s being learned.
