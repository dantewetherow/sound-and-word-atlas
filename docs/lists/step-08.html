Step 8: The e that keeps v off the end

English words don’t end in v. A silent e comes after it: have, give, live. In gave the e also makes the a long.

This list goes with Step 8: The e that keeps v off the end.

Print this list: the teacher’s notes are left off.

The words

I have a dog.
Give me the ball.
Where do you live?
She gave me a hug.
saveSave some for me.
I am five.

The contrast

The e after v can be silent, or send a vault: live is short, five is long.

Add the e

  1. hav, add e →
  2. giv, add e →
  3. liv, add e →
  4. gav, add e →
  5. sav, add e →
  6. fiv, add e →

Use it

  1. We (have or gave) two cats.
  2. Please (give or live) it back.
  3. She (gave or give) it to me yesterday.
  4. I can count to (five or fine) .

Check: not on the list

  1. dr + i + ve →drive
  2. s + o + l + ve →solve
  3. gl + o + ve →glove
  4. r + e + v →rev

For the teacher

The e in have and give is there for the v, not the vowel: the vowel stays short (Carney, p. 13). In gave and five the same e also sends a vault.

The check ends with rev: short forms and slang can end in v (rev, gov; Carney, p. 231).

Have the learner say each sentence before writing it. Six to eight words is the whole list: the convention is what’s being learned.
