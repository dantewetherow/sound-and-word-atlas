Morpheme cards: form

The parts of the form matrix on the form page, as cards that stand up. The base comes first, then the prefixes and suffixes found with it. Stand them in a row to build a word from the matrix, then try a path the matrix doesn’t list and test it.

Print the cards: eight to a page. Cut along the solid lines, fold along the dotted line, and stand each card up.

form

Tap the parts to build a path, left to right. Any box can be skipped.
