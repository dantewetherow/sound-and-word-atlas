Reasons and sources

Marking calls

Every place the marks rest on a choice that isn’t settled, in one place. Each call is drawn as the site marks it now, with the other reading beside it. The rules the marks already follow are in Definitions and on the About page.

A. Calls made with the new marks

The ə for a weak vowel and the borrowed sound written over a vowel came in on 21 November 2026. These are the choices they made that need a yes or a change.

A4 carry: still bare

What sound does the a of carry spell?

As marked now

The other reading

Before r is where Canadian speech varies most. Carried and carries follow whatever carry does.

B. Rules still to decide

B1 gi said j before a vowel

Is the gi of legion one unit, with its i sending the vault, as the ti of nation is?

As marked now

The other reading

Most speakers say two syllables, lee-jun; some say three. If you hear the i, it can't be inside the unit.

B2 an e before a vowel sends a vault

Does the e of simultaneous send the vault to the a, as an i before a vowel does?

As marked now

The other reading

Built on one word so far. Some -eous words keep the long vowel before it (spontaneous) and some don't, so check a few more before ruling.

B3 long u with no vault

Where no word ending in e is in the family, does the long u take a vault?

As marked now

The other reading

Under rule 17 (structured word inquiry), lunar has its vault from lune. Duke is in the family of ducal but changes its k to c, so the base is duc and there's no e to replace.

B4 a seam between Greek parts

Do telephone and photograph take a seam between their Greek parts?

With a seam

No seam

Both are still shown in plain letters, so neither side is the site's marking yet. Rule 17 points to the seam: structured word inquiry writes tele + phone and photo + graph, and the family pages already seam homo + phone and techno + log.

C. Earlier calls

Gathered from the code files, where they waited for a ruling: 21 open, 7 ruled, 13 settled by a later ruling. Each word is drawn as it is marked now.

open
helped
 a box over lp and a blocked vault from the e of -ed
Follows the manual's step 14, which names helped.
open
played, tried
 the e of -ed in a cone after the seam
As goes and does treat the e of -es.
open
redo, replay, refill, reread
 a long mark over the e of re-
As be- is marked in before and because.
open
wrong
 a short mark over o, and ng as a black unit
As going's ng.
open
legomorphic
 seams after leg, after o and after morph, a short e, and a long o with no vault
Seam after leg added 10 November 2026, at Dante's question. The o is the verb's ending for I, in Greek and Latin alike (leg-o, gather-I), so it is a morpheme of its own; read instead as the linking o of biology, it still sits between seams. It is long because it ends its morpheme, as in go and going, so nothing after the seam sends it a vault. The seam between o and morph joins two Greek parts, which is still open for telephone and photograph. The e is short, as in Lego, so leg closes its syllable, as lem does in lemon.
The long o with no vault follows rule 13. The seam between the Greek parts waits on B4.
open
mishap, haphazard
 s and h, and p and h, on either side of a seam, each with its own circle
The seam splits what would otherwise read as sh and ph. The ar of hazard takes the r-controlled unit of car, though it spells the er sound: a unit's second sound is marked alike, as look's oo is.
open
silly
 a box over ll and a blocked vault from y, with no seam
As middle. Silly comes from Old English gesælig, happy or fortunate (Etymonline), not from sill + y, so its double l is inherited, as the tt of pretty is. Happy has a seam and silly doesn't, though both once meant fortunate.
open
don’t
 a long mark over the o, the red apostrophe, and no seam
The apostrophe follows the I’m marking card, which says it does the same job in don’t: it stands in for the dropped o of not. The o spells its name before nt, as in old and both, so it takes a long mark and no vault.
open
six
 one circle under the x
The x spells two sounds, k and then s. Rule 1 would give it two circles, as the o of one has two. No card shows an x yet.
open
thank, think, drink
 n and k as two consonants, with no mark for the ng sound of the n
As s spelling z is unmarked. In sing, bring and long the ng spells one sound and keeps its unit, as in going.
open
its
 a seam before the s
Its is it + s, as gets is get + s. Without the seam, ts is a final cluster.
open
upon
 no seam, and the first vowel bare
As away and today are marked on the K–2 cards, since up- no longer means up. The word sum cards would put a seam after up.
21 November: its first vowel takes ə. The seam after up is still open.
open
hopped
 box on pp; the e of -ed in a cone, its reach stopped by the box
The -ed adds no syllable, so its e is silent, as on the verb cards.
open
hoped
 a seam before the e, which keeps the vault back to the o
The e of -ed takes over the silent e's job, as the i does in hoping.
open
hopper
 box on pp; a blocked vault from er
-er reaches back as -ing does, so the p doubles.
open
hopefully
 box on ll, a seam between the two l's, a blocked vault from the y
The two l's come from two parts but spell one sound, as the ee of been does. Or no box at all: the u of -ful is weak, so there's no short vowel to protect.
open
said
 a seam between ai and d
Marked now as one piece. The ai keeps a team's second sound.
open
liked
 the e after the seam in a cone, with a vault back to the i
The e of like and the e of -ed are one e. It keeps the vault, the way the vault moves to the i in making.
open
longer
 n and g as two consonants, not the ng unit
The g is sounded in longer, as it isn't in long or singer.
open
picking
 a box over ck and a blocked vault from the i of -ing
Carney treats ck as the doubled k (p. 121), so it blocks the reach the way tt does in getting.
open
fully
 a box over ll with a blocked vault from the y, and the seam after ll
Which l drops can't be seen, so full keeps its ll. The box follows smaller and spelling, where a doubled ending blocks the reach of the vowel after it.
ruled
table, bugle, title
 a vault from le back to the long vowel
As the final stable syllable card draws table and bugle.
21 November, reversing 20 November: the silent e of -le sends the vault across one consonant to a stressed vowel, and a double before -le blocks it (bubble).
ruled
notation
 notate + ion: ti as a green unit across a seam, the t black and the i red, with the vault sent from the i
Ruled 8 November 2026: the t and i spell sh together, so their bar and circle are green, as for sh, and the letters keep their own colours, so the i is red, and it still makes the a long. Until then ti was a black unit, as the create family deck draws creation.
ruled
happy, happier, happily, unhappy
 a seam after the doubled p: hap + y, as sun + y
Ruled 9 November 2026. Hap, chance or luck, isn't said on its own now, but its family shows the join: happen, hapless, mishap, haphazard. The double p alone doesn't show a seam, since rabbit and lesson double with nothing to join. Until then happy was drawn as an inherited double with no seam, as pretty is.
ruled
robin
 a short o closed by b, and a short i in -in, with no vault
As lemon and seven, and as the role cards draw robin. The i of -in is said lightly but still has the sound of the i in sit, so it takes a short mark, as in music and doing.
12 November.
ruled
robot
 a long first o, and a second o that is short
One consonant between, and the first o spells its name. The second o is short, as in hot, for most speakers; said weakly, it would be bare.
12 November.
ruled
river
 a short i closed by v, with no vault, and er as the r-controlled unit
As seven and never: a short first vowel takes the consonant into its syllable (riv·er), and er is marked as in tiger.
12 November.
ruled
today, always, away, around, before, because
 a seam inside each, as a word sum
These are marked now without a seam. Before also loses its vault, since nothing crosses the seam before a root. Ruled for before on 8 November 2026: be|fore, with no vault.
settled
silent
 the weak e of -lent is bare
The silent card puts a short mark on it; the later rule leaves a weak vowel bare.
21 November: the weak e takes ə.
settled
climb
 a long mark and no vault before mb
As find and kind.
20 November, rule 13: a long mark and no vault; no letter makes the i long.
settled
photo
 a vault from the second o to the first
As pony.
20 November: no vault.
settled
notate
 note + ate: the e drops, and the a of ate sends a vault back to the o
As making and photo.
21 November, rule 17: note/+ate, the a taking the dropped e's place.
settled
happen
 happ + en: a seam after the doubled p, and the e of -en bare
As happy. Etymonline gives happen as hap with the verb-forming -n; the e is said weakly, as in seven.
21 November: the e of -en takes ə.
settled
hapless
 hap + less: one p and no box
A consonant follows the seam, so nothing doubles. The e of -less is bare, as in hopeless.
21 November: the e of -less takes ə.
settled
gone
 a bare o and an e in a cone, as done
Gone has the short o of got for most speakers, and done has the u of up. Both are marked as borrowing vowels at step 19, since the e sends no vault. A short mark on gone's o would be the other reading.
21 November: a short o (A5).
settled
produce, conduct (the *deuk- page), drafts
 two markings each: the verb with a bare first vowel, the noun with a marked one
Stress falls on the root in the verb (proDUCE, conDUCT) and on the prefix in the noun (PROduce, CONduct). With no stress mark, the weak vowel is the only sign. Ruled 10 November 2026: the noun produce has a long mark on its o and no vault, since a vault would name a cause the letters don't supply, and capitals show stress in captions. Some speakers say a short o, which would close the syllable instead (prod·uce). The ct of duct is one consonant cluster, as the nt of went.
21 November: the verb's weak first vowel takes ə.
settled
tiger, paper, open, even, robot, silent
 a long mark on the first vowel and no vault
Changed 10 November 2026 at Dante's direction, after the noun produce: these are one part each, and no letter makes the first vowel long, so a vault would name a cause that isn't there. Until then each had a vault from the next vowel. Over, pony and photo still have that vault and wait for the same ruling.
20 November, rule 13: no letter makes these long, so no vault. Silent's e now takes ə.
settled
music, basic
 a seam before ic, and a vault from the i to the long vowel
Added 10 November 2026: muse + ic and base + ic. The i took the dropped e's place and its job, as in making. Music is from Greek mousike, of the Muses; basic is base + ic (Etymonline).
21 November, rule 17: muse/+ic and base/+ic.
settled
hopeful, hopeless, hopelessness
 the e of hope keeps its cone and its vault across the seam; the endings' vowels bare
The endings' vowels are said weakly, so they take no mark.
21 November: the endings' weak vowels take ə.
settled
wanted, started, wishes, washes
 the e of -ed and -es bare where it adds a syllable
As a weak vowel is bare elsewhere. Where the e is silent (played, goes), it stays in a cone.
21 November: the e of -ed and -es takes ə where it adds a syllable.
settled
-ful, -less, -ness, -est, -able
 weak vowels bare, ss as a doubled ending, and ble as consonant + le
-ful has one l, and the a of -able is bare, as a weak vowel is elsewhere.
21 November: the weak vowels take ə.

Ruled from A and B

Each is drawn as it is marked now.

ruled
A1
 ee over every final y
21 November: Keep the ee over a final y that spells ee (happy, city, very), with the box and blocked vault where a double stands before it.
ruled
A2
 was: ah, u, or weak and strong
21 November: u. It reads most cleanly.
ruled
A3
 put and do: one oo for two sounds
21 November: ʊ, the International Phonetic Alphabet's letter for the vowel of book, over the u of put and the oo of book; do keeps oo.
ruled
A5
 gone: still bare
21 November: A short o, as in got.
ruled
A6
 legible: a weak i
21 November: A short mark, in every -ible word: legible, eligible, reducible, intelligible.
ruled
A7
 elite: ee over a French i
21 November: The final e makes the i long and sends a vault, which lands beside the ee: elite, police, expertise.
