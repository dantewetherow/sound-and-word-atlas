How the walk works

Try it puts the marks in the learner’s hands. Pick a tool, then tap or swipe it onto the word: red for vowel letters, green for sh, th and ch, and every mark in the notation. The word’s parts can be written as a word sum, or put back together.

Show ours sets the site’s marks underneath, only the kinds the learner has used, to talk about. Nothing is scored.

Read it starts from the word in plain letters and decodes. Spell it starts from the syllables, then the sounds, then the letters. Tap or swipe through either. Under the word is its sum: tap a part to see every word that shares it.
