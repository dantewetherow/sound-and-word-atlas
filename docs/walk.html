WordsPick a word

Look up a word, then tap or swipe through it, or try marking it first. Read it starts from the word in plain letters and decodes; Spell it starts from the syllables, then the sounds, then the letters. Try it puts the marks in the learner’s hands: pick sound circles, bond bars, syllable swoops, short or long marks, cones, vowel vaults, block boxes, nice-nudges or seams, then tap or swipe them onto the word, or write its word sum. Show ours sets the site’s marks underneath to talk about; nothing is scored. Under the word is its sum: tap a part to see every word that shares it.
