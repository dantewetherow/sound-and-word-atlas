Lessons in reading and spelling, in order: the route through English spelling. Start here to learn the notation.

Open this page on the board: the words big, the notes hidden.

The route at a glance

The route has thirty-two steps in eight bands. Each mark enters with the first word that needs it, and the verb kernel runs through every band. Open a step to see its words marked, the marks it brings in, and the pages in Carney's survey that back it.

Each word is shown with its letters in colour: red for vowel letters, green for sh, th and ch, black for the rest.
