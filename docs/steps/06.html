Affixes and roots

Closed, with two consonants after the vowel, as must and spell. Each is marked as it’s said on its own; inside a word many are said weakly.

New marks

black bond bar on doubled endings (will, spell); the box comes with endings at step 14

Kernel words

has, had, did, can, will, shall, must, get, went
