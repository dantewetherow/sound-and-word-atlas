Consonant + le

New marks

none: a red bond bar and one red circle under le

Kernel words

None at this step.

Carney (1994)

le is a vowel and a consonant with the letters switched, p. 129; a short vowel before le doubles (bubble, bottle; rule #D9), p. 124; le beside al, el, il and ol, p. 433
