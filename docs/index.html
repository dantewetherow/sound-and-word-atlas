LegomorphicsSounds and Words, Assembled

Legomorphics is a set of tools for teaching how English words work: their sounds, their letters and the parts they are built from. It is made for a teacher and a class to use together, on the board. It isn’t a program that moves a learner through on their own: choose the page that fits the lesson.

It isn’t levelled by grade or age. It is for all learners, and any learner may want or need some part of it to see how English spelling works.
